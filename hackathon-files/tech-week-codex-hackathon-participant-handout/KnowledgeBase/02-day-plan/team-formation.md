# Team Formation

## Recommended Team Size

2 to 5 people.

You can participate solo if organizers allow it, but teams usually move faster when they cover product, technical, data, safety, and demo work.

## 30-Minute Formation Flow

| Minute | What To Do |
|---|---|
| 0-5 | Stand near or identify with the track you are most interested in |
| 5-12 | Share quick ideas |
| 12-20 | Form teams around the clearest problems |
| 20-25 | Narrow the scope with a TA if needed |
| 25-30 | Record team name, members, track, and project idea |

## Fast Idea Pitch

```text
Track:
User:
Problem:
What we can demo by 15:30:
Data/tool needed:
Help needed:
```

## Team Roles

One person can cover multiple roles.

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Product/story owner | User, problem, workflow, demo story |
| Technical owner | Implementation and integration |
| Data/API owner | Data, endpoints, credentials, provenance |
| Trust/safety owner | Guardrails, limitations, human review |
| Demo/submission owner | Final write-up and timing |

## Team Registration Info

Be ready to provide:

- Team name.
- Participant names.
- Track.
- Project title.
- Problem statement.
- Planned demo.
- Help needed.
