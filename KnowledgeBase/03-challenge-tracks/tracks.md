# Challenge Tracks

Choose one track. Your project can touch more than one area, but your final submission should name one primary track.

## Track 1: Improve Patient Experience

Help patients prepare, navigate, understand, follow up, or take action.

Example directions:

- Visit preparation.
- Appointment navigation.
- Follow-up task explanation.
- Benefits or care-plan comprehension.
- Patient-friendly summarization.
- Missing information reminders.

Good success metrics:

- Reduced confusion.
- Faster task completion.
- Fewer missed steps.
- Better follow-up completion.

## Track 2: Improve Encounter Experience

Help clinicians or care teams before, during, or after an encounter.

Example directions:

- Pre-visit chart summary.
- Care gap prep.
- Handoff summary.
- Draft note support.
- Visit follow-up checklist.
- Team coordination view.

Good success metrics:

- Faster prep time.
- Fewer missed context items.
- More complete handoffs.
- Less documentation burden.

## Track 3: Reduce Administrative Burden

Reduce repetitive manual work, routing, triage, review, summarization, or documentation.

Example directions:

- Inbox triage.
- Request classification.
- Draft response generation.
- Forms or document review.
- Work queue prioritization.
- Manual lookup assistant.

Good success metrics:

- Minutes saved per task.
- Reduced manual clicks.
- Higher first-pass routing accuracy.
- Fewer repeated reviews.

## Track 4: Improve Data Liquidity, Trust, And Transparency

Make data easier to exchange, inspect, validate, explain, or trust.

Example directions:

- Data provenance viewer.
- Missing-field detector.
- Synthetic FHIR record explorer.
- Terminology mapping helper.
- Data quality explainer.
- Audit-friendly AI output summary.

Good success metrics:

- Faster data validation.
- Better traceability.
- Reduced ambiguity.
- Higher reviewer confidence.

## Track Selection Tip

Pick the track where your demo can show the clearest before/after workflow.
