The Problem

Referral coordinators spend 15–30 minutes per referral manually answering three questions: Is this schedulable? What's missing? What do I do next? Multiply that across dozens of daily referrals and you get bottlenecks, delays, and patients who fall through the cracks.

Primary User Referral Coordinator / Admin Staff
Setting Specialist Practice on athenahealth
Root Cause Unstructured, incomplete incoming referrals
Impact Bottlenecks, leakage, delayed care
What We Built

Five outputs. One agent. Zero manual triaging.

📊

Readiness Score

Classifies every referral as Ready to Schedule, Missing Information, Needs Authorization, or Needs Clinical Review — instantly.

⚠️

Missing Info Detection

Automatically flags what's absent — diagnosis codes, imaging, insurance, referring NPI, patient contact — before it reaches a coordinator's queue.

🏥

Specialty Routing

Recommends the right specialty and explains why — "Likely cardiology, imaging required before scheduling" — so nothing lands in the wrong queue.

✉️

Outreach Draft

Generates an editable follow-up message to the referring office requesting missing information. Human-reviewed before sending.

🚨

Urgency Flagging

Identifies Routine vs. Urgent vs. Stat referrals so coordinators prioritize their day correctly — not by gut feel.

📋

Human Approval Checklist

Every AI recommendation requires coordinator sign-off before action. Built-in transparency and accountability at every step.

⚠️ The Manual Triage Gap — Where Referral Ready Plugs In
No structured completeness check
athenaOne files the referral but doesn't automatically tell you what's missing — demographics, imaging, diagnosis codes, insurance. Coordinator has to know what to look for.
Manual readiness review = bottleneck
The "Referral readiness review" step is 100% human. Every referral requires a coordinator to read, assess, and decide — 15–30 min of cognitive work per packet.
Outreach loop causes leakage
When info is missing, coordinators manually draft outreach to referring offices. Referrals stall, patients wait, and some fall through entirely — no automated escalation or aging alerts.
✅ Referral Ready fills exactly this gap
We sit between athenaOne intake and the readiness review — automatically parsing, scoring, and generating outreach so coordinators only review, not investigate.

How AI Is Actually Helping

The agent loop, explained

Not magic. A structured reasoning pipeline on top of Azure OpenAI.

1

Parse unstructured referral text

LLM reads the raw referral — fax-style, free text, any format — and extracts structured fields.

Input: "Pt referred for chest pain eval, see attached" → Extracted: patient, diagnosis, urgency, provider
2

Check completeness against requirements

Agent cross-references extracted fields against Da Vinci DTR authorization requirements and FHIR coverage data.

Missing: ICD-10 code, imaging report, prior auth number
3

Score readiness + route

Generates a readiness classification, confidence score, urgency flag, and specialty routing recommendation.

Score: MISSING INFO · Confidence: 72% · Route: Cardiology
4

Generate outreach draft

Produces an editable follow-up message to the referring office. Coordinator reviews and approves before any action is taken.

Draft: "Dear Dr. Kim's office, we are missing imaging results for..."

Trust & Transparency

Responsible AI by design

👁️

Human always in the loop

Every AI recommendation requires coordinator approval before any action is taken. No autonomous sending or scheduling.

📊

Confidence scores shown

The agent surfaces its confidence level on every analysis so coordinators know when to trust and when to double-check.

🔒

Synthetic data only

Built and demonstrated entirely on Synthea synthetic records and CMS public datasets — zero real patient PHI.

📝

Explainable outputs

Every missing-info flag and routing recommendation includes a plain-language reason — not a black box.

🛡️

Fallback rules

If Azure OpenAI is unreachable, the system falls back to deterministic rule-based analysis with a visible safety note.

✏️

Editable before sending

All outreach drafts are editable. Coordinators review and personalize every message before it leaves the system.
